## lata name and logo

The lata name, the tin can logo and its variations (`assets/icons/svg`, `assets/icons/png`,
`assets/icons/lata.ico`, `assets/icons/lata.icns`, `extension/icons`) are not covered by the GPL.
Using them needs written permission from the lata authors.

> All rights reserved. © 2026 the lata authors.

If you fork this repository, remove the lata name and logo from your fork.

## Project Zomboid material

These belong to The Indie Stone and are used to refer to the game. They are not covered by the
GPL:

- `assets/banner.jpg`, a screenshot from the game's Steam store page.
- `assets/instance-icons/`, item pictures from the game.
- `assets/screenshots/`, which also show pictures from Workshop mods that belong to their authors.

lata is a fan project, not affiliated with The Indie Stone.
